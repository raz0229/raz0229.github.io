Account Deletion Information

Taash Online

1. How to delete your account

You can delete your account directly in the app:

  1. Open the Settings screen from the home tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete account".
  3. Confirm that you want to delete your account.

You will be signed out immediately and your account will be scheduled for deletion.

If you run into any problem, you can also request deletion by emailing sheraztech229@gmail.com from the address linked to your account.

2. What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, the following is removed from the Service:

  • Your profile, display name and email address.
  • Your virtual-coin balance, avatars and all in-app items.
  • Your game history, match records and leaderboard entries.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You will not be able to sign in to the deleted account again.

3. What may be retained

A small amount of information may be kept for a limited period where we are required to by law or for legitimate business reasons, such as:

  • Records of purchases, for refund and tax obligations.
  • Records needed to prevent fraud, cheating or abuse.
  • Information required by applicable law or legal proceedings.

Any retained data is stored securely, is used only for those purposes, and is deleted as soon as those obligations end.

4. How long it takes

Deletion is processed automatically and is usually completed within a few days. In some cases — for example when a deletion is reviewed for compliance reasons — it can take up to 30 days.

We will not process purchases or wins under an account that has been deleted.

5. Before you delete

Please consider the following before deleting your account:

  • Virtual coins and avatars cannot be transferred to another account and are not refundable.
  • You will lose access to any pending or in-progress matches.
  • You will need to create a brand-new account if you want to play again afterwards.
